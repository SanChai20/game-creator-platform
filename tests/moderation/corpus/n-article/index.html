10 Tips to Win at Chess

Chess rewards patience. Here are ten habits that strong players share, from the first move to the last.

1. Control the centre

Pawns on e4 and d4 give your pieces room. Knights before bishops, and castle early.

2. Develop every piece

Each move in the opening should bring a new piece into play. Do not move the same piece twice without a reason.

3. Look for checks, captures and threats

Before every move, list your opponent's forcing moves, then your own.

4. Trade when ahead

When you are up material, simplify. Every trade brings you closer to a winning endgame.
